# 🇧🇿 Belize Interactive Map

Explore the tropical geography of Belize through our highly optimized, interactive SVG map. Perfect for dashboards, data visualization, and regional targeting across all 6 districts.

## 🌍 Country Overview

Belize is a small nation on the Caribbean coast of Central America, known for its vast barrier reef, hundreds of cayes, and rich Maya heritage. It is the only country in the region with English as its official language.

### 🎨 Design & Flag Inspiration
The visual identity of this map pack is inspired by the **National Flag of Belize**, which features a royal blue field with red stripes and the national coat of arms.
* **Hover State:** We utilize a vibrant translucent blue (`rgba(0, 63, 135, 0.35)`) to represent the blue field of the flag, symbolizing the Caribbean Sea and the sky.
* **Borders:** Rendered in Deep Navy (`#1e293b`) to ensure maximum contrast and a modern, premium aesthetic.

<Admonition type="info" title="How it works">
Every `<path>` element in the generated SVG automatically receives `data-code` (e.g., `"BZBZD"`) and `data-name` (e.g., `"Belize District"`). You can listen for clicks on the parent container to instantly build drill-down experiences, filtering, or regional detail views.
</Admonition>

## 💻 How to Render It

Implementing the **Belize** map in your project takes just a few lines of code. Since every map is generated as a plain SVG string, the exact same approach works in **React**, **Vue**, **Angular**, **Svelte**, and **vanilla JavaScript**.

<Admonition type="tip" title="One pattern, every framework">
Generate the SVG with <code>createMap()</code>, inject it into a container, and listen for clicks using the automatic <code>data-code</code> and <code>data-name</code> attributes.
</Admonition>
