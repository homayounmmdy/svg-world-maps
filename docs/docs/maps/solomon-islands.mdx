# 🇸🇧 Solomon Islands Interactive Map

The Solomon Islands is an Oceanian country consisting of nearly 1,000 islands, famous for its World War II historical sites, incredible marine biodiversity, and traditional Melanesian culture.

## 🌍 Overview

The Solomon Islands is an Oceanian country consisting of nearly 1,000 islands, famous for its World War II historical sites, incredible marine biodiversity, and traditional Melanesian culture.

### 🎨 Design & Flag Inspiration
The visual identity of this map pack is inspired by the **National Flag of Solomon Islands**.
* **Hover State:** We utilize a vibrant translucent color (`rgba(0, 51, 160, 0.35)`) to represent the national identity and heritage.
* **Borders:** Rendered in Deep Navy (`#1e293b`) to ensure maximum contrast and a modern, premium aesthetic.

## 💻 How to Render It

Implementing the **Solomon Islands** map in your project takes just a few lines of code. Since every map is generated as a plain SVG string, the exact same approach works in **React**, **Vue**, **Angular**, **Svelte**, and **vanilla JavaScript**.

<Admonition type="tip" title="One pattern, every framework">
Generate the SVG with <code>createMap()</code>, inject it into a container, and listen for clicks using the automatic <code>data-code</code> and <code>data-name</code> attributes.
</Admonition>
