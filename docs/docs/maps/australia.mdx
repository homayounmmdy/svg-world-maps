# 🇦🇺 Australia Interactive Map

Experience the vast, sun-burnt continent of Australia through our highly optimized, interactive SVG map. Perfect for dashboards, data visualization, and regional targeting spanning states and territories.

## 🌍 Country Overview

Australia is an island continent situated between the Indian and Pacific oceans. It features a wildly diverse landscape, ranging from the tropical rainforests of the north and the vast red deserts of the Outback to the highly populated coastal urban centers.

### 🎨 Design & Flag Inspiration
The visual identity of this specific map pack is inspired by the **National Flag of Australia**, a Blue Ensign featuring the Union Jack and the Commonwealth Star.
* **Hover State:** We utilize a vibrant translucent red (`rgba(228, 0, 43, 0.35)`) to represent the red elements of the Union Jack and stars, symbolizing courage and the iconic red earth of the Outback.
* **Borders:** Rendered in Deep Navy (`#1e293b`) to ensure maximum contrast and a modern, premium aesthetic.

<Admonition type="info" title="How it works">
Every `<path>` element in the generated SVG automatically receives `data-code` (e.g., `"AUNSW"`) and `data-name` (e.g., `"New South Wales"`). You can listen for clicks on the parent container to instantly build drill-down experiences, filtering, or regional detail views.
</Admonition>

## 💻 How to Render It

Implementing the **Argentina** map in your project takes just a few lines of code. Since every map is generated as a plain SVG string, the exact same approach works in **React**, **Vue**, **Angular**, **Svelte**, and **vanilla JavaScript**.

<Admonition type="tip" title="One pattern, every framework">
Generate the SVG with <code>createMap()</code>, inject it into a container, and listen for clicks using the automatic <code>data-code</code> and <code>data-name</code> attributes.
</Admonition>
