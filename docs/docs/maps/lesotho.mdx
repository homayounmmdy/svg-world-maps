# 🇱🇸 Lesotho Interactive Map

Lesotho is an independent, landlocked kingdom entirely surrounded by South Africa, known as the 'Kingdom in the Sky' due to its high elevation and the spectacular Maloti Mountains.

## 🌍 Overview

Lesotho is an independent, landlocked kingdom entirely surrounded by South Africa, known as the 'Kingdom in the Sky' due to its high elevation and the spectacular Maloti Mountains.

### 🎨 Design & Flag Inspiration
The visual identity of this map pack is inspired by the **National Flag of Lesotho**.
* **Hover State:** We utilize a vibrant translucent color (`rgba(0, 33, 115, 0.35)`) to represent the national identity and heritage.
* **Borders:** Rendered in Deep Navy (`#1e293b`) to ensure maximum contrast and a modern, premium aesthetic.

## 💻 How to Render It

Implementing the **Lesotho** map in your project takes just a few lines of code. Since every map is generated as a plain SVG string, the exact same approach works in **React**, **Vue**, **Angular**, **Svelte**, and **vanilla JavaScript**.

<Admonition type="tip" title="One pattern, every framework">
Generate the SVG with <code>createMap()</code>, inject it into a container, and listen for clicks using the automatic <code>data-code</code> and <code>data-name</code> attributes.
</Admonition>
