# 🇦🇹 Austria Interactive Map

Experience the alpine geography of Austria through our highly optimized, interactive SVG map. Perfect for dashboards, data visualization, and regional targeting across all 9 federal states.

## 🌍 Country Overview

Austria is a landlocked country in Central Europe, dominated by the Eastern Alps. Known for its rich musical heritage, alpine villages, and the Danube River, it borders eight countries.

### 🎨 Design & Flag Inspiration
The visual identity of this map pack is inspired by the **National Flag of Austria**, one of the oldest flag designs in the world, featuring red-white-red horizontal stripes.
* **Hover State:** We utilize a vibrant translucent red (`rgba(239, 51, 64, 0.35)`) to represent the red stripes of the flag, symbolizing bravery and strength.
* **Borders:** Rendered in Deep Navy (`#1e293b`) to ensure maximum contrast and a modern, premium aesthetic.

<Admonition type="info" title="How it works">
Every `<path>` element in the generated SVG automatically receives `data-code` (e.g., `"ATVIE"`) and `data-name` (e.g., `"Vienna"`). You can listen for clicks on the parent container to instantly build drill-down experiences, filtering, or regional detail views.
</Admonition>

## 💻 How to Render It

Implementing the **Austria** map in your project takes just a few lines of code. Since every map is generated as a plain SVG string, the exact same approach works in **React**, **Vue**, **Angular**, **Svelte**, and **vanilla JavaScript**.

<Admonition type="tip" title="One pattern, every framework">
Generate the SVG with <code>createMap()</code>, inject it into a container, and listen for clicks using the automatic <code>data-code</code> and <code>data-name</code> attributes.
</Admonition>
